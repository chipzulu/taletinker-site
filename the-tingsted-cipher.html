The Tingsted Cipher

By C.P. Helweg

Kindle preorder open

A literary science-fiction thriller of first contact, survival, and an impossible connection to Earth's past.

After a disaster on Callisto, geologist Latimer Boreaux joins a deep-space expedition aboard the Amaterasu. On a world of deserted cities, the survivors uncover an alien script that links their predicament to Earth's distant history.

As supplies run low and trust fractures, deciphering that script may be their only chance of survival.
