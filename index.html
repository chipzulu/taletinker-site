About the author

At the edge of the map.

C.P. Helweg writes across the line between lived experience and fiction, with an eye for the moment when ordinary reality becomes something sharper.

His life began moving early: Atlantic sailing as a teenager, later work aboard merchant ships in the Far East, a career in computer technology and software architecture, and service at sea as the commander of a Coast Guard patrol vessel.

Maritime experience and technology both find their way into the work. Whether the setting is a storm deck, a barren moon, or somewhere much harder to name, the aim is the same: make the reader stand inside it.

C.P. Helweg is the publishing name of Christian P. Koch.

Influences & instigators

Why I Write

I write fiction the way Michael Strunge wrote poetry—not for commentary, but for collision. Not for what a story should do, but for what it can make you feel in the gut.

Patrick Süskind gave me atmosphere. John Irving, the weight of story. Kurt Vonnegut, the smirk. David Foster Wallace showed me how meaning can fold in on itself and still connect.

But the heartbeat is Strunge: language that does not ask permission. It claims space.
